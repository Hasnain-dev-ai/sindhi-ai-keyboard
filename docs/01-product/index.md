## 📖 About This Part

Part 01 defines the **product layer** of Sindhi AI Keyboard.

The Foundation established **why the project exists**. This Part now establishes **what we are building**.

It translates the project's vision and principles into a structured product definition covering the keyboard itself, its multilingual experience, AI capabilities, Skill Pack ecosystem, offline-first behavior, community use cases, accessibility, sustainability, and long-term ecosystem direction.

The product must remain grounded in the project's central promise:

> **A smooth, reliable, ad-free, Sindhi-first experience that remains useful even when connectivity and device resources are limited.**

# 🧩 Sections vs. Chapters

Not every product topic requires a separate Markdown file.

A chapter may contain multiple:

* Sections.
* Subsections.
* Tables.
* Requirements.
* Examples.
* Product rules.
* Acceptance criteria.

The project will create a new chapter only when a topic is substantial enough to deserve its own document.

This keeps the documentation structured without creating unnecessary fragmentation.

---

# 🎯 Product Documentation Principles

Product documentation should:

1. Preserve the project's Sindhi-first identity.
2. Prioritize reliable typing over optional intelligence.
3. Treat offline capability as a first-class requirement.
4. Protect the typing experience from interruptions.
5. Follow the strict Skill Pack model.
6. Consider device capability before enabling resource-heavy features.
7. Preserve the Windows Sindhi layout as the reference typing standard.
8. Support Sindhi, Urdu, and English as core languages.
9. Keep the fundamental experience ad-free.
10. Treat privacy as a product requirement.
11. Design advanced capabilities as modular features.
12. Keep future Sindhi SDK requirements in mind.
13. Avoid allowing future platform ambitions to destabilize the core keyboard.
14. Document significant product decisions clearly.
15. Validate major features against real community needs.

# 🚦 Product Priority

When product requirements conflict, the product follows this general priority:

```text
Reliable Typing
      ↓
Low Latency
      ↓
Offline Capability
      ↓
Privacy & Security
      ↓
Sindhi Language Quality
      ↓
Device Compatibility
      ↓
Skill Packs
      ↓
Useful AI
      ↓
Community Accessibility
      ↓
Advanced / Sustainable Services
```

A feature should not be accepted merely because it is technically impressive if it compromises the experience above it.

# 🌍 Long-Term Product Direction

        ↓

Offline AI

        ↓
Voice Intelligence
        ↓
Domain Skill Packs
        ↓
Advanced AI Services
        ↓
Sindhi SDK
        ↓

This is a direction, not a requirement that every capability ship simultaneously.
