## 🕌 About This Project

**Sindhi AI Keyboard** is a long-term technology initiative under the **Sindhi AI Platform** vision of **Solangi Group of Companies (SGoC)**.

The project begins with a keyboard, but the vision is significantly larger than a conventional keyboard application.

The goal is to create a complete language and AI platform that helps Sindhi-speaking communities communicate, write, translate, understand, create, learn, work, and interact with modern technology in their own language.

The keyboard is therefore designed as the **first major gateway into the Sindhi AI Platform**.

---

# 🎯 Vision

Our long-term vision is to build a world-class Sindhi-first AI platform that brings modern language technology to people regardless of:

- 🌍 Where they live
- 📶 How good their internet connection is
- 📱 How powerful their phone is
- 🎓 Their education level
- 🏢 Their profession
- 🌾 Whether they live in a city or village
- 💼 Whether they work in an office, field, business, school, hospital, government department, or another environment

The platform should make advanced technology accessible without requiring users to abandon their language.

---

# ❤️ Mission

Our mission is to:

- Strengthen digital Sindhi.
- Make Sindhi typing fast, natural, and modern.
- Provide Sindhi, Urdu, and English in one unified keyboard.
- Preserve established Sindhi typing standards and muscle memory.
- Bring useful AI capabilities directly to users.
- Make core functionality available without continuous internet access.
- Keep the keyboard completely free from advertisements.
- Avoid interruptions, intrusive pop-ups, and unnecessary UI during typing.
- Protect users' private text and sensitive information.
- Support communities with limited connectivity and lower-end devices.
- Build AI capabilities for many fields instead of limiting the platform to a single industry.
- Create a sustainable foundation that can eventually support community participation and open-source development.

# ⌨️ Keyboard Philosophy

## Typing Is Sacred

The keyboard is the most latency-sensitive part of the entire platform.

AI must never make basic typing worse.

### The fundamental rule:

> **AI may enhance typing, but AI must never interrupt typing.**

This means:

* ❌ No blocking network request while typing
* ❌ No loading spinner over the keyboard during ordinary typing
* ❌ No advertisements
* ❌ No intrusive pop-ups
* ❌ No unexpected keyboard replacement
* ❌ No unnecessary animation causing lag
* ❌ No cloud dependency for basic typing
* ❌ No large model forced into every device

Instead:

```text
Key Press
   ↓
Immediate Local Processing
   ↓
Immediate Text Output
   ↓
Optional AI Enhancement
```

The typing path must remain independent from expensive AI operations.

---

# 🧠 Engineering Rule

A feature should not be added merely because it is technically possible.

Every feature should answer:

1. Does it solve a real user problem?
2. Does it improve the user's experience?
3. Can it work offline when appropriate?
4. Does it preserve privacy?
5. Does it respect low-end devices?
6. Does it avoid interfering with typing?
7. Does it justify its storage, memory, battery, and maintenance cost?
8. Can it be modularized?
9. Can it be tested?
10. Can the community realistically benefit from it?

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.
